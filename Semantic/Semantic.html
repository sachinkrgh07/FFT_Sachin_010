    • The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) program with specializations in Data Science, General, Generative AI, and Game Designing is designed to deliver advanced computing knowledge along with domain-specific expertise aligned to emerging industry trends. The program follows a comprehensive, application-driven approach, enabling students to build strong conceptual understanding and practical proficiency in core areas such as programming (Java, Python), data structures, algorithms, database management systems, cloud computing, and software engineering. From the early stages, students progressively develop expertise in their chosen domains: Data Science focuses on data analysis, statistical modeling, visualization, and big data technologies. Generative AI emphasizes deep learning, large language models, natural language processing, and intelligent systems. Game Designing covers game design, graphics and animation, UIUX, and immersive technologies like VRAR. The General track offers flexibility to explore multiple computing domains. The program integrates experiential learning through hands-on labs, design projects, and capstone work, enabling students to develop real-world solutions such as AI applications, data-driven systems, and interactive digital experiences.
